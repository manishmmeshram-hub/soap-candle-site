What goes in

How we make them

Slow on purpose

  1. DAY 1

    Blend and pour

    Oils and butters are warmed, mixed with lye solution, and poured into moulds with the herbs, clays and powders for that batch.

  2. DAY 2

    Cut by hand

    Once firm, each loaf is cut into bars and stamped.

  3. WEEKS 1 TO 6

    Cure

    Bars rest on open racks while the water evaporates. This is what makes them mild and long-lasting.

  4. CANDLE DAY

    Melt, scent, pour

    Soy wax is melted, the fragrance is stirred in, and the wax is poured into jars at about 55 °C. Candles set for a week before they ship.
